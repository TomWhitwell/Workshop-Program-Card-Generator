Program Card Generator

Upload SVG artwork and get customised Workshop Computer Program Cards. These can be ordered at JLC PCB for relatively affordable rates with a minimum order of 8 cards.

1) Upload SVG

SVG specifications
  • Either upload 1 SVG (used in all 4 positions) or 4 SVGs (one per slot)
  • Download the design kit (ZIP) for Illustrator and Inkscape starter files, artwork preparation instructions, and a guide to checking and using the generated Gerber files to order your cards.
  • Use a 19 × 11 mm SVG page. You can use the starter files or create your own artwork. Any layer names are fine; all visible artwork is included. Hide or delete reference outlines and guide layers before saving.
  • Filled paths and standard vector shapes are supported. Convert live text and strokes to paths before uploading.
  • Artwork beyond the page edge is clipped. Always check the proof and generated Gerber files before manufacturing.
